# O.N.E.Tech - Enterprise AI Infrastructure Platform

## Vision
Governance-first AI infrastructure company serving GCC and Cyprus-based enterprises. Transform fragmented operations into intelligent ecosystems through AI orchestration, governance frameworks, and operational intelligence.

**Target Audience:** Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Cyprus - Energy, Industrial, EPC, Real Estate, Financial Services, Forex Brokers

## Design
Premium enterprise platform inspired by Palantir, Anduril, Scale AI, Accenture Industry X.

**Color System:**
- `--background: 220 15% 8%` (dark charcoal) - Main background
- `--foreground: 0 0% 98%` (near white) - Primary text
- `--primary: 152 69% 31%` (emerald green #00B67A) - CTAs, accents, trust elements
- `--accent: 152 69% 41%` (bright emerald) - Hover states, highlights
- `--muted: 220 15% 15%` (dark gray) - Cards, panels
- `--border: 220 15% 25%` (subtle gray) - Component borders
- `--card: 220 15% 12%` (card background) - Glass panels

**Typography:**
- Headings: Inter (bold, modern, enterprise-focused)
- Body: Inter (clean, professional, readable)

**Style Direction:** Enterprise platform - dark sophisticated aesthetic, glass morphism panels, infrastructure diagrams, AI orchestration visuals, executive-level design. Generous whitespace, subtle animations, premium feel.

## Features
Complete enterprise-grade website for AI infrastructure company:

**Core Pages:**
1. **Homepage** - Hero (governance-first), Trust Bar, Problem, Platform Stack, Solutions, Industries, AI Governance, GCC/Cyprus positioning
2. **Solutions** - Operational Infrastructure, Revenue Infrastructure, Real Estate Infrastructure, Forex Infrastructure, Workforce Intelligence
